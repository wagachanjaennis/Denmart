Good-ad checklist

  • Open with the one big thing you want visitors to remember.
  • Use genuine product photos; keep the main headline to a few words.
  • Use one action: scan the QR code or visit denmart.co.ke.
  • Show the date clearly for opening-day or seasonal campaigns.
  • Preview every scene and verify prices/promises before publishing.
