What changes — and what never does

The feeder operates as an online display layer. It does not import an API's prices or stock into Denmart.

01Online photos and barcodesWhen API mode is active, matching feed fields are used. Missing API photos remain blank rather than silently showing an older local photo.
02Admin-created products take priorityProducts added through Denmart's Products page or CSV import can keep their own barcode/photo as a local override.
03POS and offline stay localCashier scanning, offline catalogue sync, prices, inventory, order matching and checkout continue to use Denmart's database.
04Graceful outage behaviourThe last successful feed stays cached. A failed refresh is reported here and does not erase the last good feed or your current database.
